3. Add alias, lane, prompt-profile reference/handler compatibility, token/count/
   price/unit/finish-reason capability, policy, and digest validation.

Validate stable aliases, immutable versions, portable providers/models,
explicit profile bindings, forbidden defaults/inheritance/overrides, disabled
entries, incomplete policies, and absence of profile bodies or secrets in

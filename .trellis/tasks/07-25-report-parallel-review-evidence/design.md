# Parallel Review Evidence Reporting Design

## Boundary

This child projects durable plan/child/aggregate evidence into bounded reporting
and effectiveness-ready identities. It does not adjudicate findings, recommend
models, or change reviewer configuration.

Reports preserve exact head, slot, candidate, actual provider/model, selection
source, status, cost/unknown, completion policy, deferred/recovery state, and
finding-channel attribution. They carry review, assurance, and gate outcomes
independently plus the gate reason and projection revision. Truncation retains
those outcomes, limitations, projection ownership, and recovery references
before optional detail.

## Rollback

Historical multi-reviewer evidence remains readable after fan-out is disabled.
Reporting schema versioning prevents old evidence from authorizing new work.
